# Privacy Policy — The Collector (Research Beta)

Last updated: this beta's release.

Contact: **lunchboxfortwo@gmail.com**

## What The Collector is

The Collector is a **research-purpose beta** app. We built it to study how well automated art appraisal works. It is not a finished product.

## What data we collect

When you use the app, you can submit:

- a **photo** of an artwork (taken with the camera or picked from your device),
- an **optional description** you type,
- optionally, an **internet link** to the artwork.

When you submit, the photo, description, and link are **sent to the developer's server** to produce an appraisal. The app cannot produce a result without sending this data.

## How we use the data

We use submitted data to:

- operate the app and return an appraisal to you,
- study and improve the beta's accuracy.

We do **not sell** your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with third parties except what is technically required to run the service.

## Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your submitted photos and descriptions. Email **lunchboxfortwo@gmail.com** and we will remove them.

## No guarantee of quality or availability

- Our server is **not production-grade**. It may be slow, unavailable, or taken offline at any time.
- Appraisal results are **estimates produced by an experimental system**. They are not guarantees of value and should not be relied on for any purchase, sale, insurance, or tax decision.

## Changes

This policy may change as the beta develops. Material changes will be reflected in this file.
